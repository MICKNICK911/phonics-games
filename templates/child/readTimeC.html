📖 HOW TO PLAY

1️⃣ Tap any hand (hour / minute / second) on the clock → it turns RED (selected).

2️⃣ Use ⬅️ (anti-clockwise) and ➡️ (clockwise) buttons to move the selected hand.

3️⃣ Tap the same hand again to deselect.

4️⃣ Match the clock to the target time and press ✅ SUBMIT.

5️⃣ Easy mode: hours + minutes (no seconds/AMPM). Hard mode: full seconds + AM/PM.

💡 Each level starts with a random clock position (never matching the target).

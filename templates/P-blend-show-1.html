How to Play

  • Tap one letter, then tap another to highlight all letters between them
  • Tap a single letter twice to select just that letter (not a valid blend)
  • Click "Check Selection" to verify if your selection is a valid blend in this word
  • You must find every blend in the word before moving on
  • You get 3 tries per blend — finding a blend refreshes your tries

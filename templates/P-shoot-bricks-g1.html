❔ How to Play

Create words that contain the target sound to fire at the bricks.

  • Type a word using the on-screen keyboard or your physical keyboard.
  • Move the shooter one column at a time with ◀ and ▶.
  • Press FIRE (or Enter) to shoot.
  • Valid words destroy one brick in the aimed column.
  • Invalid or repeated words add a red penalty brick.
  • Clear all bricks to win. Six penalties and you lose.

📖 How to Play

  1. Tap adjacent letters that form a single sound (e.g., "sh", "ee", "p").
  2. Click "Found Sound" to lock them – they get a unique colour.
  3. Cover every letter (except silent letters – they make no sound).
  4. Click "Check Sounds" to verify your answer.
  5. ✅ Correct → next word. If you grouped a silent letter, you'll get a gentle reminder.
  6. ❌ Wrong → you lose one ❤️ and try again.
  7. After 3 wrong attempts → game over. Finish all 10 words to win!

Press Esc or tap outside to close
